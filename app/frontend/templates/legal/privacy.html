Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SpawnRadar collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect the information needed to operate the service, including:

  • Account information such as your email address, sign-in details and session information needed to keep your account secure.
  • Game, messaging and outreach data that you add to your account.
  • Subscription and account access status that we maintain in our own systems.
  • Payment, transaction and billing details for paid subscriptions processed through Paddle.
  • Basic site activity logs, such as page visits and product interaction events to improve the service, security and user experience.
  • Support or feedback messages you send to us.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain and improve SpawnRadar.
  • Authenticate users and secure accounts.
  • Process subscriptions, billing and account support.
  • Understand product usage and improve the user experience.
  • Investigate abuse, fraud or violations of our terms.

3. Cookies and session technologies

We may use cookies or similar session technologies to keep you signed in, maintain account security and support core product functionality.

4. Service providers

We use third-party providers where necessary to run the service, including infrastructure hosting, authentication, email delivery and billing. Paddle processes paid subscriptions and related payment details and Google sign-in may be used for authentication if you choose that login option.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with service providers when needed to operate SpawnRadar, comply with the law or protect the service and its users.

6. Data retention

We retain account and product data for as long as needed to provide the service and maintain business records. We may retain limited information longer where required for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

8. Your choices

You can contact us to request account deletion or to ask questions about your information. Depending on where you live, you may also have additional privacy rights under applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date on this page.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@spawnradar.com.
