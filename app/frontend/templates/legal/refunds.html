Refund Policy

Effective date: March 21, 2026

This page explains how trials, cancellations, and refund requests are handled for SpawnRadar subscriptions.

1. Free trial

If your plan includes a free trial, you can cancel before the trial ends and you will not be charged for the first billing period.

2. Subscription renewals

Paid subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. If you do not want to be charged for the next billing period, you must cancel before the renewal date.

3. Refunds

Because SpawnRadar is a software subscription with immediate access to product features, we do not generally offer prorated or routine refunds for partial billing periods.

That said, if you believe you were charged in error or forgot to cancel shortly after renewal, email us within 7 days of the charge and we will review the request in good faith. Refund requests are handled case by case and may be denied except where refund rights are required by law.

If a refund is approved, it will be issued back to the original payment method through Paddle where possible.

4. Chargebacks and disputes

If you have a billing issue, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it quickly. Unnecessary chargebacks may result in account suspension while the issue is reviewed.

5. Contact

Refund and billing requests can be sent to contact@spawnradar.com.
