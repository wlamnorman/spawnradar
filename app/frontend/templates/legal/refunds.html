Refund Policy

Effective date: March 21, 2026

SpawnRadar subscriptions are sold through Paddle, our merchant of record. Buyer cancellations, withdrawal rights, and refunds are handled in line with Paddle's Buyer Terms and Refund Policy.

1. Free trial

If your plan includes a free trial, you can cancel before the trial ends and you will not be charged for the first billing period.

2. Subscription cancellations

Paid subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. You can cancel at any time through Paddle's buyer portal or the link in your billing emails. Your cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged again after that.

3. Refunds and withdrawal rights

Refunds, statutory withdrawal rights, and refund eligibility are handled according to Paddle's Refund Policy and applicable consumer protection law. Eligible buyers may request a refund within 14 days of the transaction in line with Paddle's Refund Policy and any applicable mandatory consumer rights.

Refunds are processed by Paddle to the original payment method where possible. Processing times depend on your payment provider.

4. How to request a refund

To request a refund, use the "View receipt" or "Manage subscription" link in your Paddle email receipt, or visit paddle.net. If you need product-specific help, you can also contact us at contact@spawnradar.com.
